## Primary Distribution Channels
1. **Direct Teacher Signup** — Google Ads targeting "vocabulary app for teachers"
2. **Parent App Store** — iOS/Android organic + ASO
3. **School Procurement** — outbound to curriculum coordinators
4. **Clever/ClassLink Marketplace** — SSO integration drives district adoption
5. **EdTech Review Sites** — Common Sense Media, EdSurge listings
6. **TikTok/Instagram** — Student-made pronunciation challenge clips (viral loop)
7. **Email Nurture** — Free teacher → school upsell sequence

## Viral Mechanics
1. **Streak sharing** — "I'm on a 14-day VocaQuest streak!" shareable card
2. **Badge unlock share** — "I just earned Elite Orator badge!" social card
3. **Boss Battle challenge** — Student challenges classmate via link
4. **Parent report share** — Parent forwards weekly progress to grandparents
5. **Teacher referral** — Refer another teacher, get 1 free month
